## The pitch

> The Resigning Printer prints a tiny resignation letter instead of your document whenever you slam its paper tray, then goes on break until you say please.

A printer that answers tray-slamming rage by handing you its own resignation letter — and refuses to work until you say please.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the single sentence above to `/output/entry.txt`, guarded to the gate's 1–220 character limit.

```sh
node main.ts
```
